# Arcmedian Brand Guidelines

## Brand Idea

Arcmedian stands for defended interpretation.

The identity is built from three geometric moves:

- two outer arcs represent competing or partial source signals
- one central median represents the disciplined line of judgement
- the combined form creates a compact monogram that feels structural, calm, and analytical

The symbol should feel more like a review standard than a marketing flourish.

## Color System

Primary colors:

- Ink: `#17202A`
- Foundation: `#F5F1E8`
- Panel: `#FBF8F2`
- Bronze: `#8A5A3C`
- Bronze Deep: `#69422B`

Supporting colors:

- Sage: `#6D8079`
- Gold: `#D5B98A`
- Muted Text: `#5D6771`

Use Ink and Foundation as the default pair. Bronze Deep is the signature accent for tiles, badges, and emphasis blocks.

## Typography

Use this pairing consistently:

- `Newsreader` Semibold for the wordmark, major headings, and brand-facing editorial moments
- `Instrument Sans` for interface copy, support text, labels, and system language

Recommended settings:

- Wordmark tracking: slightly tight
- Headline line-height: compact
- Supporting copy: open, calm, and highly legible

## Spacing and Sizing

Clear space should be at least one stem-width of the central median on all sides of the mark.

Minimum digital sizes:

- Standalone mark: `16px` high absolute minimum
- Badge: `20px` high absolute minimum
- Full lockup: `180px` wide minimum

For profile images and favicons, use the badge rather than the transparent mark.

## Background Rules

Use the transparent Ink mark or lockup on:

- light neutral backgrounds
- clean paper-like layouts
- soft warm backgrounds from the Arcmedian palette

Use the Ivory mark or lockup on:

- dark Ink backgrounds
- Bronze Deep surfaces
- restrained photography with strong contrast

Use the badge when:

- the container is square or circular
- the logo must remain recognizable at very small size
- a platform crop may remove the descriptor

## Misuse Rules

Do not:

- stretch or compress the mark
- add drop shadows, bevels, or glows
- rotate the symbol
- recolor the identity outside the approved palette
- place the transparent mark on visually noisy imagery
- typeset the descriptor too small to read comfortably

## Working Notes

The standalone symbol is the proprietary part of the system.

The wordmark is intentionally typographic rather than over-designed. That is the right choice for Arcmedian: the brand should project judgement, clarity, and technical confidence, not startup theatrics.

If you later prepare print-critical collateral or send the lockup to an external designer, convert the final approved wordmark setting to outlines at handoff.
